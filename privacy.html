AXIM Backup Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026.

AXIM Backup is a private backup utility used by its owner to transfer encrypted backup data to Google Drive.

Google user data

The application requests only the Google Drive access needed for backup operations. Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Data storage

Backup data is encrypted before it is stored in Google Drive. OAuth credentials are used only to authenticate backup operations.

Data retention and deletion

Backup files remain in the owner's Google Drive until deleted by the owner or by the configured backup retention policy. Access can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google Account permissions.

Contact

For questions about this application, use the contact email configured on the Google OAuth consent screen.
